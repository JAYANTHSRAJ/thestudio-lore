SOBHA ROYAL PAVILION

"A symphony of space and story"

Rooted in warmth, tradition, and everyday elegance, this home is envisioned as a lived — in sanctuary for a family of five that values culture and comfort in equal measure. Every detail — be it handcrafted woodwork, cane accents, handloom fabrics, art frames, or timeless lighting – echoes a narrative of thoughtfulness and personal expression.

From a quiet reading nook to a cozy children’s room adorned with joyful prints, and a serene pooja space framed by stately columns, each area is curated to reflect character and calm. The design balances heritage with functionality, embodying Studio Lore’s classic ethos with grounded modernity.

MEDLEY B35

"Design evolving with trends"

Step into the dynamic world of an Instagram influencer, where the interior design serves as a versatile backdrop for an ever-evolving lifestyle. From the foyer table with its hand-painted ceramic jar drawer pulls to the strategically placed open shelves showcasing curios, each element is carefully curated to reflect personal style. The ‘Kathakali’ motif prints on the master bedroom wardrobe shutters add a distinctive touch, while the ochre backsplash tiles and paint accents provide a striking contrast to the warm wood tones, creating a space that adapts and grows with its owner’s unique journey.
